Privacy policy

Activity Collector is used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

It asks Google for one permission: to create calendars and manage events on the calendars it creates. It cannot see any other calendar. It uses that permission only to add the owner's check-ins to a calendar it created.

All data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's Google account. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Google user data obtained through this permission is not used to develop, improve or train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
